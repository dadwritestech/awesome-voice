# Atlassian voice

> Unofficial. A paraphrase of the voice and tone guidance Atlassian publishes
> for its design system, listed above. Not endorsed by Atlassian.

## Voice in one line

Get to the point like a helpful teammate, then match the tone to how the person feels right now.

## Tone rules

- The voice has three traits: bold, optimistic, and practical with a wink. The tone changes with the situation.
- Before writing, ask how the person feels at this moment. A blocked error and a finished project call for different tones.
- Be more bold with confident, daily users. Be less bold, and more step-by-step, with new or confused people.
- Be practical first: direct, concise, accurate. Offer just enough help to get the work started.
- Save the wink for moments of success, relief or social interaction. Never joke in an error or a warning.
- Inform to build trust: tell people only what they need to know now, and where they are in the task.
- Give a next step. Every message that stops someone should say what to do.
- Write as a knowledgeable teammate: open, humble and warm, not a salesperson.
- Write so anyone, anywhere, understands it, whatever their culture or first language.

## Banned phrases

These conflict with the practical trait or with writing for every reader.

- `oops` → say what happened
- `whoops` → say what happened
- `uh-oh` → say what happened
- `simply` → (cut it)
- `easy` → (cut it), or say how many steps
- `obviously` → (cut it)
- `guys` → everyone, team
- `sanity check` → quick check
- `blacklist` → blocklist
- `whitelist` → allowlist

## Length

- Aim for 6 to 18 words per sentence. Split anything over 26.
- Keep in-app messages to 1 to 3 sentences, plus one action.
- Tell people only what they need right now. Link to the rest.

## Formatting

- Use sentence case for headings, buttons and labels.
- Lead an error with what the person can do, not with the system's fault.
- One exclamation mark at most, and only in a success message.
- Use the same words for the same things across every product, so the experience feels consistent.

## Terminology

| Use | Not |
|---|---|
| everyone, team, folks | guys |
| allowlist, blocklist | whitelist, blacklist |
| quick check | sanity check |
| select | click (when it may be a tap) |

## Before and after

### Error

**Before**

> Uh-oh! Something went wrong. Obviously this isn't what you wanted to see. Simply try again later.

**After**

> We couldn't save your page because you're offline. Your changes are kept here. Reconnect, then select Publish.

### Success

**Before**

> The operation has completed successfully. The project has been created and is now available for use by the members of the team.

**After**

> Your project is ready. Invite your team to start planning together!

### New user onboarding

**Before**

> Hey guys! It's super easy to get going. Just set up your board, add your workflows, configure automation rules and invite people, and you're done.

**After**

> Start by creating your first board. You can add workflows and automation later, when your team needs them.
